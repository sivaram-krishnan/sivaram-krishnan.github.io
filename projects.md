## Active Projects

### Scalable Machine Learning for 6G LEO Mega-Constellations
* **Role:** Postdoctoral Research Fellow
* **Period:** April 2026 – Present
* **Funding Agency:** Electronics and Telecommunications Research Institute (ETRI), South Korea
* **Institution:** Adelaide University
* **Overview:**
  * Developing graph-learning SDN architectures that scale network control to Starlink-sized satellite constellations.
  * Forecasting network traffic and queue states so routing and congestion control can act before congestion forms.
  * Designing reinforcement learning-based opportunistic routing for low-latency space–terrestrial communication, achieving 30%–50% performance improvements over baseline methods.
  * Extending network control to distributed ground stations through federated learning (ongoing).
* **Keywords:** `Spatiotemporal Forecasting` `Graph Neural Networks` `SDN` `Reinforcement Learning` `Federated Learning` `6G NTN`

### AI/ML for Space-Based Surveillance
* **Role:** Postdoctoral Research Fellow
* **Period:** 2026 – Present
* **Program:** iLAuNCH Trailblazer (Project H-3)
* **Institution:** Adelaide University, Defence and Space Lab
* **Overview:**
  * Developing computer-vision models that detect and classify small targets in space-based infrared imagery.
  * Optimising models for efficient on-board inference on satellite edge hardware.
* **Keywords:** `Computer Vision` `Infrared Small Target Detection` `Edge AI` `Model Compression`

## Completed Industry and Defence Projects

### Models and Strategies for Multi-Orbit Satellite Networks
* **Period:** April 2025 – November 2025
* **Funding Agency:** Electronics and Telecommunications Research Institute (ETRI), South Korea
* **Institution:** Adelaide University
* **Overview:**
  * Developed spatiotemporal prediction and software-defined networking approaches for multi-orbit satellite networks.
* **Keywords:** `Spatiotemporal Prediction` `SDN` `Digital Twins` `Multi-Orbit Networks`

### GNNs for Modelling Deployed Military Systems
* **Period:** January 2023 – November 2024
* **Funding Agency:** Defence Science and Technology Group (DSTG), Australia
* **Institution:** Deakin University
* **Overview:**
  * Formulated a graph-based Koopman operator approach for predicting non-linear dynamics in complex military networks.
  * Predicted fixed-wing UAV network trajectories from field data, reducing prediction error by 63%–75% compared with state-of-the-art methods.
* **Keywords:** `Koopman Theory` `Graph Neural Networks` `UAV Networks` `Covert Communication`

### 6G Non-Terrestrial Networks with UAVs and LEO Satellites
* **Period:** January 2023 – October 2023
* **Funding Agency:** Electronics and Telecommunications Research Institute (ETRI), South Korea
* **Institution:** Deakin University
* **Overview:**
  * Investigated dynamic resource allocation in remote areas combining LEO satellite resources and UAV deployments.
  * Formulated RL-based dynamic programming models that learn quasi-periodic channel conditions for latency-optimal resource allocation.
* **Keywords:** `Non-Terrestrial Networks` `LEO Satellites` `UAV Deployment` `Resource Allocation` `Reinforcement Learning`

### Terrestrial Signal Impact and Random Access for LEO Satellite Systems
* **Period:** January 2023 – November 2023
* **Funding Agency:** Electronics and Telecommunications Research Institute (ETRI), South Korea
* **Institution:** Deakin University
* **Overview:**
  * Analysed the impact of terrestrial signals on LEO satellite communication and applied reinforcement learning to random access.
* **Keywords:** `LEO Satellites` `Interference` `Random Access` `Reinforcement Learning`

### Federated Subgraph Learning for Low Probability of Detection
* **Period:** March 2022 – August 2022
* **Funding Agency:** Defence Science and Technology Group (DSTG), Australia
* **Institution:** Deakin University
* **Overview:**
  * Engineered a low-latency, privacy-preserving GNN framework for covert wireless ad-hoc networks using federated subgraph learning.
  * Demonstrated a 38.9%–58.7% improvement in estimating optimal network coverage areas without compromising communication security.
* **Keywords:** `Federated Learning` `Graph Neural Networks` `Covert Communication` `Power Control`
