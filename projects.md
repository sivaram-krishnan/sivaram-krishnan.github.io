## Active Projects

### GNN and Associated Technologies for 6G LEO Mega-Constellations
* **Role:** Lead Researcher / Postdoc
* **Period:** April 2026 – Present  
* **Funding Agency:** Electronics and Telecommunications Research Institute (ETRI), South Korea  
* **Institution:** Adelaide University  
* **Overview:** 
  * Developing Software-Defined Network (SDN) architectures for satellite mega-constellations (e.g., Starlink scale) using Graph Neural Networks (GNNs).
  * Proposing Reinforcement Learning (RL)-based opportunistic routing protocols for low-latency space-terrestrial communications, achieving 30%–50% performance improvements over baseline methods.
* **Keywords:** `Reinforcement Learning` `Satellite Mega-Constellations` `GNN` `SDN` `6G NTN`

## Completed Industry & Defence Projects

### GNNs for Modelling Deployed Military Systems
* **Period:** Jan 2023 – Nov 2024  
* **Funding Agency:** Defence Science and Technology Group (DSTG), Australia  
* **Institution:** Deakin University  
* **Overview:** 
  * Formulated a novel graph-based Koopman operator approach for non-linear dynamics prediction in complex military networks.
  * Successfully predicted fixed-wing Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) network trajectories using field data collected at Cultana, Australia, reducing prediction error by 63%–75% compared to state-of-the-art methods.
* **Keywords:** `Koopman Theory` `Graph Neural Networks` `UAV Networks` `Covert Operations`

### 6G Non-Terrestrial Networks with UAVs and LEO Satellites
* **Period:** Jan 2023 – Oct 2023  
* **Funding Agency:** Electronics and Telecommunications Research Institute (ETRI), South Korea  
* **Institution:** Deakin University  
* **Overview:** 
  * Investigated dynamic resource allocation in remote areas combining Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite resources and UAV deployments.
  * Formulated RL-based dynamic programming models to learn quasi-periodic channel conditions for latency-optimal resource allocation.
* **Keywords:** `Non-Terrestrial Networks` `LEO Satellites` `UAV Deployment` `Resource Allocation` `RL`

### Federated Subgraph Learning for Low Probability Detection
* **Period:** Mar 2022 – Aug 2022  
* **Funding Agency:** Defence Science and Technology Group (DSTG), Australia  
* **Institution:** Deakin University  
* **Overview:** 
  * Engineered a low-latency, privacy-preserving GNN framework for covert wireless ad-hoc networks using federated subgraph learning.
  * Demonstrated a 38.9%–58.7% improvement in estimating optimal network coverage areas without compromising communication security.
* **Keywords:** `Federated Learning` `GNN` `Covert Communication` `Power Control`
