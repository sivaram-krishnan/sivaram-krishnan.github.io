## About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at **Adelaide University**, working on machine learning for wireless and satellite networks. My work applies spatiotemporal modelling, time-series forecasting and sequential decision-making to problems such as routing and congestion control in satellite mega-constellations, in collaboration with ETRI (South Korea). I also develop computer-vision models for space-based surveillance at the Defence and Space Lab, as part of the iLAuNCH Trailblazer program.

I completed my Ph.D. at Adelaide University in 2026, supervised by Prof. Jinho Choi, A/Prof. Jihong Park and A/Prof. Brian Ng.

---

## Research Interests

* **AI for Wireless and Satellite Networks:** forecast-driven routing and congestion control, software-defined control of mega-constellations, covert communication.
* **Spatiotemporal and Graph Learning:** time-series forecasting, Koopman-based models of network dynamics, federated learning.
* **Sequential Decision-Making:** reinforcement learning for routing and access in satellite–terrestrial networks.
* **Computer Vision for Space:** infrared small-target detection and efficient on-board inference.
